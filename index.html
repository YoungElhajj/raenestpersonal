Frequently asked questions

Is Raenest a bank?

Raenest is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services are provided by banking partners. Card services are provided by Raenest's technology partners, authorised by Visa and Mastercard to issue cards operating on their networks.

How long does it take to get my bank account?

You’ll receive access to your account once you’ve submitted the required information and completed verification.

What's the fee for receiving in my bank account?

The fees for incoming transfers are determined on a case-by-case basis. For a detailed breakdown of charges, please consult our full fee schedule here.

What is the Raenest USD Visa Card and how is it related to Raenest?

The Raenest USD Visa Card is a standalone payment card issued by Rain, a Visa Principal Member, and powered by Visa. Raenest provides the platform where users can access and manage the card.

The card is separate from other Raenest services, such as global accounts, stablecoins, currency conversion, money transfers and investments.

Can I use my USD account on Upwork, Deel, Fiverr, or Payoneer?

Yes. You can use your USD account to receive payments from platforms that support USD bank deposits.
